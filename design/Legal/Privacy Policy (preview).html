Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Autocut is a video editing app operated by [operator name] ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Autocut app and its server, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email [contact email].

The short version

  • To cut your videos, the takes you send (and any music, photos or clips you add to an edit) are uploaded to our server, processed there, and deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Transcription runs on our own server. We don't send your videos or voice to third-party AI services.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • You can delete your account, and everything we hold about you, from Settings › Delete account in the app.

What we collect

Account information

Your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and, if you allow it, the name they share with us.

Profile and setup answers

Things you choose to tell us: your name, your TikTok handle, the language you record in, your answers during setup (what kind of creator you are, your goal, and roughly what you pay an editor), and the plan you pick (Starter, Pro or Ultimate, monthly or yearly). We use these to set up your account, suggest a plan, and give you what your plan includes.

Videos, audio and transcripts

When you tap Send, Autocut uploads the takes you recorded or picked to our server. The server listens to the audio to produce a transcript with word timings, finds pauses, filler words and repeated takes, and prepares the video for editing and export. Video, audio, transcripts and exported files are stored on our server for up to 7 days and then deleted automatically. Copies you save to Photos, and the edits stored in the app, stay on your device until you delete them.

Music, photos and clips you add

If you add music, a photo or a video overlay to an edit, that file is uploaded with your edit so the server can put it into your export. It's kept and deleted on the same 7-day schedule as your takes.

How many edits you've made

Plans include a number of edits a month, so our server counts how many edits your account starts each month (stopped or failed edits aren't counted). This is a number tied to your account; it doesn't contain your videos. It's kept until you delete your account.

Stored on your device

Your edits, batches you're putting together, settings and cached previews are stored in the app on your device. They aren't sent to us except when you send a video to be cut or exported.

Device permissions

Autocut asks for your camera and microphone (to record takes), and for permission to add videos to your photo library (to save exports). Picking videos or photos from your library (to cut them, or to add them as overlays) gives the app only the items you choose. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

Technical information

Our server keeps basic logs (such as request times and errors) to keep the service running and secure. These logs don't include your video content.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: sign you in, cut and caption your videos, let you edit and export them, and give you the features and number of edits your plan includes.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix problems.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your videos, voice or transcripts to train AI models, and we don't use them for advertising.

Who processes your data for us

We use a small number of providers who process data only on our instructions:

  • Supabase – accounts and sign-in.
  • Railway – hosting for the Autocut server, where uploads are processed and stored temporarily.
  • Apple and Google – only if you choose to sign in with them, and Apple for App Store purchases.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded takes, transcripts and exports on our server: up to 7 days.
  • Account and profile information, including your plan and this month's edit count: until you delete your account.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You can update your profile and settings in the app, and delete your account at any time from Settings › Delete account. Deleting your account removes your account, profile, edit count, uploads and cuts from our systems; videos you already saved to Photos stay on your device. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may also have the right to access, correct or receive a copy of your data, or to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email [contact email].

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and every request to our server must carry your sign-in token, so each account can only reach its own videos. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Autocut is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll tell you in the app.
