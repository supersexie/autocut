FAQ

Questions, answered.

Contact us →
What does Autocut cut, exactly?

Long pauses (dead air), filler words like "um" and "uh", stutters, and retakes: when you say a line again, it keeps your last attempt and cuts the earlier one. Then it captions every word that's left.

Can I change what it cut?

Yes. Your cut opens in a full editor: trim, split, change speed, restore anything, edit captions, and add text, music, photos and clips. Undo is always there.

Is there a watermark? What quality does it export?

No watermark. Exports go up to 4K and 60 fps, and save straight to Photos, with one tap to TikTok or Instagram.

How long can my videos be?

Record up to 20 minutes in the app, or import videos up to 1 GB. Most videos are cut in about a minute.

Which languages does it understand?

English and Spanish.

Is Autocut on Android?

Not yet. Autocut is for iPhone and iPad.

Do my videos stay in the app?

Yes. Your cuts stay in Autocut until you delete them, and your videos are kept on our servers until you delete your account, so you can come back and edit again. Delete your account anytime in Settings.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. Autocut runs on iPhone and iPad, with the editor laid out for the bigger screen.

Can I edit more than one video at once?

Yes. In Batch, line up several videos (each from one or more clips) and tap Edit all. On Pro and Ultimate you can also schedule them for later; they're cut even with your phone off.

What counts as an edit?

Each video Autocut cuts for you. Stopped or failed cuts don't count, and your edits reset at the start of every month.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In the app, under Your plan. If you subscribed through the App Store, you can also cancel in your iPhone's Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you offer refunds?

Purchases made through the App Store are refunded by Apple: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. For anything else, contact us.
