Privacy Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

Autocut is a video editing app operated by {{OPERATOR}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you use the Autocut app and its servers, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email {{EMAIL}}.

The short version

  • The videos you send to Autocut (your raw takes and the cut, captioned versions we make) are kept until you delete your account, so they're there whenever you want to edit again.
  • If you agree, we also use your videos to improve Autocut and its AI, for example how well it hears words and where it cuts. You can say no, or change your mind at any time in Settings › Help improve Autocut's AI.
  • A few companies process data for us: Cloudflare (stores videos), Deepgram (turns speech into text), Supabase (accounts and our database), Railway (runs our servers), Whop and Apple with RevenueCat (payments). They're listed below.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • You can delete your account, and the videos and data we hold about you, from Settings › Delete account.

What we collect

Account information

Your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and, if you allow it, the name they share with us.

Profile and setup answers

Things you choose to tell us: your name, your TikTok handle, the language you record in, your answers during setup (what kind of creator you are, your goal, and roughly what you pay an editor), and the plan you pick. We use these to set up your account, suggest a plan, and give you what your plan includes.

Videos, audio and transcripts

When you tap Send, Autocut uploads the takes you recorded or picked. We keep the original takes exactly as you sent them, and the videos we make from them: takes joined together, previews for the editor, and every export. To find what to cut, we turn the speech into a transcript with word timings and look for pauses, filler words and repeated takes. All of this is stored until you delete your account. Copies you save to Photos, and the edits stored in the app, stay on your device until you delete them.

Music, photos and clips you add

If you add music, a photo or a video to an edit, that file is uploaded so we can put it into your export, and it's kept with your videos.

Payments

If you subscribe, the payment is handled by Apple (in the app) or by Whop (on the web, in the US). We don't see or store your card details. We receive a record of what you bought: the plan, when it started and renews or ends, and a reference to the purchase, so we can give you that plan.

What you agreed to

We record which version of these terms and this policy you agreed to and when, and whether you allowed your videos to be used to improve Autocut and its AI, and when you changed that.

How many edits you've made

Plans include a number of edits a month, so we count how many edits your account starts each month (stopped or failed edits aren't counted).

Stored on your device

Your edits, batches you're putting together, settings and cached previews are stored in the app on your device.

Device permissions

Autocut asks for your camera and microphone (to record takes), and for permission to add videos to your photo library (to save exports). Picking videos or photos from your library gives the app only the items you choose. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings at any time.

Technical information

Our servers keep basic logs (such as request times and errors) to keep the service running and secure. These logs don't include your video content.

How we use it

  • To provide Autocut: sign you in, cut and caption your videos, let you edit, re-edit and export them, keep them available to you, and give you what your plan includes.
  • To improve Autocut and its AI, if you agree: we may use your videos, audio and transcripts to develop, train, test and evaluate the systems that cut and caption videos, and to improve the app. People on our team may review samples for this. If you turn this off, we stop using your videos for this from then on.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix problems.
  • To bill you for the plan you choose.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your videos for advertising, and we don't sell them.

Who processes your data for us

These companies process data only to provide their service to us, under agreements that require them to protect it:

  • Cloudflare (R2 storage): stores your videos and the files we make from them.
  • Deepgram: receives the audio of your videos to turn speech into text with word timings. We ask Deepgram not to use your audio to train its own models.
  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in, and our database (your cuts, plan and settings).
  • Railway: runs the servers that cut and export your videos.
  • Whop: payments made on the web (US only).
  • Apple and RevenueCat: payments made in the app (RevenueCat keeps track of App Store subscriptions for us). Apple and Google also handle sign-in if you choose them.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

If the business changes hands

If Autocut, or the company behind it, is sold, merged, reorganised or goes through a similar change, your information, including your videos, may be transferred to the new owner as part of that deal. The new owner would have to use it as this policy describes, unless they tell you otherwise and give you a choice where the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Your videos, transcripts, edits and exports: until you delete your account.
  • Account, profile, plan, purchase records, edit counts and what you agreed to: until you delete your account, except purchase records we must keep longer for tax or accounting.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.

When you delete your account, we delete your videos and data from our systems. If you allowed your videos to be used to improve Autocut's AI, improvements already made to our systems from them can't be separated out and may remain, but your videos themselves are deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can update your profile and settings in the app, turn AI improvement on or off in Settings, and delete your account at any time from Settings › Delete account (videos you already saved to Photos stay on your device). Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, US state privacy laws such as the CCPA in California, or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, to withdraw consent, or to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email {{EMAIL}}.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Videos are stored privately and reached only through short-lived links made for your account, and every request to our servers must carry your sign-in token. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Autocut is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

International transfers

We and the companies above may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll ask you to review and agree to them in the app.
