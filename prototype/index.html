A preview of the iPhone app's screens and flow. The real app runs on the phone with your own video; this page plays a made-up sample take so you can click through every screen.

  1. 1Create: tap Import a video or Record now.
  2. 2Processing: cuts land on the timeline in red as dead air, ums and retakes are found.
  3. 3Cut ready: the edit plays with captions. Tap 1:10 → 0:17 to see every cut and why.
  4. 4Cuts and You tabs: past cuts and cutting settings.

The video area is a placeholder animation and the transcript is sample text. In the app, the same screens show your actual footage, cut on your iPhone.
